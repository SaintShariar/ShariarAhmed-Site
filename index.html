About

Founder energy. Public-service backbone.

I’m an entrepreneur who self-starts high-impact projects — products and systems designed so underprivileged families can move forward with clearer tools, fairer access, and real opportunity.

Years in New York City and Atlanta government taught me how to ship reliable, scalable solutions and how to build trust with the people who depend on them. I bring that same discipline to founder work: listen hard, ship thoughtfully, and stay accountable to the community.
